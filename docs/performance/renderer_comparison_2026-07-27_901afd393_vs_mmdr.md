## Stage Attribution

The follow-up stage run used the same long preset. Separate Criterion runs explain the small
end-to-end difference from the full-suite checkpoint.

| Fixture | Stage | Merman | mmdr | Ratio |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `requirement_medium` | parse | 5.65 us | 7.91 us | 0.71x |
| `requirement_medium` | layout | 136.03 us | 47.99 us | 2.83x |
| `requirement_medium` | render | 141.20 us | 13.92 us | 10.14x |
| `requirement_medium` | end-to-end | 300.39 us | 72.38 us | 4.15x |
| `mindmap_medium` | parse | 12.17 us | 8.28 us | 1.47x |
| `mindmap_medium` | layout | 92.74 us | 31.57 us | 2.94x |
| `mindmap_medium` | render | 63.82 us | 34.07 us | 1.87x |
| `mindmap_medium` | end-to-end | 165.17 us | 74.36 us | 2.22x |

Requirement is not parser-bound. A source audit found that Merman measures and prepares rich
Markdown/HTML labels during layout, then repeats part of that work during Mermaid-compatible SVG
emission. An operation-scoped prepared-label artifact is the highest-value semantics-preserving
experiment; it still requires an A/B benchmark and DOM parity gates.

Mindmap's largest ratio is in layout, but copying mmdr's radial heuristic would change the product.
A fair layout experiment must first select the same algorithm on both sides, such as an explicitly
supported tidy-tree lane.

## Comparability Limits

- Merman's end-to-end benchmark exercises the public one-shot helper and strict render-model parse.
  mmdr reuses `Theme` and `LayoutConfig` outside each iteration and calls its permissive parser.
  The current lane is useful for public convenience-path latency, not for claiming identical
  reusable-engine or strict-parser throughput.
- Merman targets Mermaid semantic and SVG DOM parity. mmdr emits a smaller behavioral surface for
  several families. Raw latency is therefore not a quality-adjusted implementation ranking.
- `xychart_medium` is 303 bytes in Merman (12 categories and two 12-point series) but 115 bytes in
  mmdr (4 categories and two 4-point series). `treemap_medium` also differs. Their raw timings are
  retained below as measured coverage but excluded from every ratio and family geomean.

## Quality and Coverage Caveat

- Timings include only successful renders for each runner. Missing or errored fixtures reduce coverage; they are not folded into ratios.
- Merman/mmdr ratios and family geomean columns include only byte-identical fixture inputs.
- `merman` is parity-focused and should still be paired with SVG DOM/resvg comparison gates before using performance numbers as a release signal.
- `mermaid-rs-renderer` has different goals and coverage. A faster partial renderer is not equivalent to a parity-compatible renderer.
- The corpus records expected quality gates per fixture; this harness currently records those expectations but does not run DOM or raster comparisons.
